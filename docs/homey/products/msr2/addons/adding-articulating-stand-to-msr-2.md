# Adding Articulating Stand To MSR-2

You'll swap the back of your sensor for the articulating stand backplate, then seat the stand's ball in its mount.

1\. Unplug your MSR-2 from power and remove the back.

![Back of the MSR-2 with the Apollo lettered backplate in place](/assets/msr-2-add-co2-pic-1.jpg)

![Holding the MSR-2 and lifting the backplate off with a thumb](/assets/msr-2-add-co2-pic-2.jpg)

![MSR-2 with the backplate removed and set aside, showing the circuit board](/assets/msr-2-add-co2-pic-3-1.jpg)

2\. Align the new backplate that came with the articulating stand addon and gently push down on two corners near the articulating ball mount then the other two corners until it seats flat.

![Pressing the articulating stand backplate onto the MSR-2 near the ball mount](/assets/msr-2-stand-pic-1.jpg)

![MSR-2 with the articulating stand backplate seated flat](/assets/msr-2-stand-pic-2.jpg)

3\. Align the ball inside the articulating mount and then push down until it's fully seated.

![Pushing the articulating stand ball into the socket on the MSR-2 backplate](/assets/msr-2-stand-pic-3.jpg)
