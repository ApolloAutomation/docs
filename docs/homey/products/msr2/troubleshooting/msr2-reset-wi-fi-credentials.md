# Reset Wi-Fi Credentials on your MSR-2

1\. Plug the device into power.

2\. Long press the Boot button (the button on the right) for 10 seconds

![MSR-2 in its case with a tool pointing at the right opening for the boot button](/assets/msr2-boot-button-pic-1.jpg)![Close-up of the MSR-2 case side with a tool at the right boot button opening](/assets/msr2-boot-button-pic-2.jpg)![MSR-2 circuit board with a tool pointing at the button labeled Boot, next to RST](/assets/msr2-boot-button-pic-3.jpg)

3\. The Wi-Fi credentials should be reset and it will broadcast its hotspot again.

4\. Connect to the hotspot titled "Apollo MSR2 Hotspot" and enter your new Wi-Fi details!
