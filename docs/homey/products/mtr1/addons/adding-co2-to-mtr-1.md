# Adding CO<sub>2</sub> To MTR-1

1\. Unplug your MTR-1 from power and remove the stand if attached.

![Back of the MTR-1 with the stand attached and the USB-C port visible](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-1.jpg)![Back of the MTR-1 with the stand removed, showing the backplate](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-2.jpg)![Lifting the backplate off the MTR-1 with the stand set aside](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-3.jpg)![MTR-1 with the backplate removed, showing the circuit board, backplate, and stand](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-4.jpg)

2\. Flip the MTR-1 upside down and gently tap the case on the table and your MTR-1 should slide out of the case.

![MTR-1 board slid out of its case, showing the HLK-LD2450 radar and main board](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-5.jpg)

3\. With the case removed, identify the bottom right corner of where the small black mezzanine connector lives. You will see an "X" indicating which corner needs to be lined up with the SCD40 CO<sub>2</sub> sensor. There is an X on the SCD40 sensor's PCB as well, you can see it in the image below. There is also a "gold dot" in the top left of the SCD40 when oriented properly.

![Close-up of the MTR-1 black mezzanine connector with the X mark at its bottom right](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-6.jpg)![SCD40 CO2 module next to the MTR-1 connector with matching X marks on both boards](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-7.jpg)

4\. Gently push the SCD40 CO<sub>2</sub> module onto the black mezzanine connector.

![Pressing the SCD40 CO2 module onto the MTR-1 mezzanine connector](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-8.jpg)![Side view of the SCD40 CO2 module seated on the MTR-1 board](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-9.jpg)

5\. Slide the case back over the MTR-1, making sure that the case has the hole for the LUX Sensor facing the right direction (to the right).

![Sliding the case back over the MTR-1 board](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-10.jpg)

6\. Flip the MTR-1 back over to prepare to put the back case on.

![Back of the MTR-1 in its case with the board visible, ready for the backplate](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-11.jpg)

7\. Gently press the backplate of the MTR-1 case onto the MTR-1 until it lightly snaps into place. Please do not force it, if necessary please repeat steps above until it seats in the case without any force required.

![Pressing the backplate onto the MTR-1 until it snaps into place](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-12.jpg)![Pressing a corner of the backplate to seat it on the MTR-1](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-13.jpg)

8\. Put the stand back on if applicable.

![Reassembled MTR-1 with the stand attached](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-14.jpg)

9\. Please refer to our [CO<sub>2</sub> calibration guide](https://wiki.apolloautomation.com/homey/products/general/calibrating-and-updating/co2-calibration/ "CO2 Calibration") to calibrate the CO<sub>2</sub> module after installation. This is required for all CO<sub>2</sub> addons purchased from us separately from a product (such as buying it to addon to an existing Apollo device).
