# MTR-1 Zone Mapper tool

The Apollo MTR-1's LD2450 mmWave sensor tracks up to three people across the room. With the Zone Mapper tool you turn that map into rooms within a room: a desk, a sofa, a bed, each with its own occupancy and its own flow cards in Homey.
