# Reset Wi-Fi credentials on your PLT-1

You can clear the Wi-Fi details saved on your device and connect it to a new Wi-Fi network through its hotspot.

1\. Plug the device into power.

2\. Long press the Boot button for 10 seconds

![Side of the PLT-1 with the boot button opening circled](/assets/plt-1-boot-button-pic-1.png)

3\. The Wi-Fi credentials should be reset and it will broadcast its hotspot again.

4\. Connect to the hotspot titled "Apollo PLT1 Hotspot" and enter your new Wi-Fi details!
