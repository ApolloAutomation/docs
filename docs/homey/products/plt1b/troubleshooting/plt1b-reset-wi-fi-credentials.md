# Reset Wi-Fi Credentials on your PLT-1B

1\. Plug the device into power.

2\. Long press the Boot button for 10 seconds

![Open PLT-1B case with a tool pointing to the boot button on the PCB near the USB-C port](/assets/plt-boot-mode-pic-2.jpg)

![Tool inserted into the boot button opening on the side of the closed PLT-1B case](/assets/plt-boot-mode-pic-4.jpg)

3\. The Wi-Fi credentials should be reset and it will broadcast its hotspot again.

4\. Connect to the hotspot titled "Apollo PLT-1 Hotspot" and enter your new Wi-Fi details!
