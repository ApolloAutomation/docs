# Teardown of TEMP-1

Follow these steps to take your sensor apart and put it back together.

1\. Gently pry open the case lid as shown in the image below.

![Flat tool prying the lid off the TEMP-1 case at the slit along the top edge](/assets/temp-1-pry-open.jpg)

![TEMP-1 case with the lid removed, showing the circuit board inside](/assets/temp-1-lid-off.jpg)

2\. Once the lid is removed either lift the PCB out or gently dump the pcb out by turning it upside down.

![Fingers lifting the TEMP-1 circuit board out of the case](/assets/temp-1-lift-pcb-out.jpg)

![Fully disassembled TEMP-1 with the lid, empty case, and circuit board laid out separately](/assets/temp-1-teardown-fully.jpg)

3\. The teardown is complete!

![TEMP-1 lid sliding onto the case, with the RGB LED visible in the bottom left corner](/assets/temp-1-align-lid.jpg)

4\. Make sure during reassembly that the RGB LED is in the bottom left as shown in the image below then press on the lid as shown below until it snaps into place!

![Finger pressing the lid onto the TEMP-1 case until it snaps into place](/assets/temp-1-press-on-lid.jpg)
