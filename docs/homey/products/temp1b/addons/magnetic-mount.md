# TEMP-1B Magnetic Mount

The TEMP-1B comes with an optional magnetic mount which can be used to secure the TEMP-1B to your fridge or grill as shown below.

![Correct TEMP-1B mount on a cool grill side shelf next to a crossed-out mount on the hot grill lid](/assets/temp1b-magnetic-mount-proper-mounting-explained-resized.png)

1\. To use your magnetic mount place it with the magnet facing upwards as shown:

![Empty TEMP-1B magnetic mount lying flat with the magnet facing up](/assets/temp1b-magnetic-mount-pic-1.jpg)

2\. Align the lid with the hole in the top right and place your TEMP-1B inside the magnetic mount and gently press down.

![Top view of the TEMP-1B seated in the magnetic mount with the lid hole in the top right](/assets/temp1b-magnetic-mount-installed-pic-1.jpg)

![TEMP-1B seated in the magnetic mount, seen from the 3.5mm port side](/assets/temp1b-magnetic-mount-installed-pic-2.jpg)

3\. It will sit flush as shown in the image below.

![TEMP-1B standing on end with the magnetic mount sitting flush against the case](/assets/temp1b-magnetic-mount-installed-pic-3-1.jpg)

4\. Affix your TEMP-1B to the outside of a fridge or a safe cool place on the grill.

![TEMP-1B in its magnetic mount attached to the outside of a fridge](/assets/temp1b-magnetic-mount-safe-outside-fridge-mounting.jpg)

![TEMP-1B in its magnetic mount on a grill side shelf with the food probe cable running into the grill](/assets/temp1b-magnetic-mount-safe-grill-mounting.jpg)

!!! danger "Do not leave your sensor outside or let it get wet!"

    The TEMP-1B should not be left outside for long periods of time or allowed to get wet. You will need to use another case around your TEMP-1B if there will be high moisture content in the air or if it is expected to rain.
