# Teardown Of TEMP-1B

1\. Gently pry open the case lid as shown in the image below.

![Prying up the TEMP-1B case lid with a thin metal tool held under the edge](/assets/temp-1b-pry-open.jpg)

![TEMP-1B case with the lid removed, showing the circuit board inside](/assets/temp-1b-lid-off.jpg)

2\. Once the lid is removed either lift the PCB out or gently dump the pcb out by turning it upside down.

![Lifting the TEMP-1B circuit board out of the open case](/assets/temp-1b-lift-pcb-out-1.jpg)

![TEMP-1B case lifted off the board, showing the battery holder underneath](/assets/temp-1b-remove-case.jpg)

3\. The teardown is complete!
