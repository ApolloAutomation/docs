# Reset Wi-Fi credentials

Here you clear the saved Wi-Fi credentials so you can enter new Wi-Fi details through the device's hotspot.

1\. Plug the device into power.

2\. Press and hold the Boot button for 10 seconds.

![Tool pointing to the AIR-1 boot button, the right of two buttons beside the USB-C port](/assets/air-1-boot-button.jpg)

3\. The Wi-Fi credentials should be reset and it will broadcast its hotspot again.

4\. Connect to the hotspot titled "Apollo AIR-1 Hotspot" and enter your new Wi-Fi details!
