# General Tips

## Sticky Pads

Your BTN-1 comes with two sets of 4 sticky pads or feet to stick on the bottom of the BTN-1 itself or on the optional BTN-1 Mount addon. These are great for setting the device on your desk!

![Peeling clear sticky pads and pressing them onto the four corners of the BTN-1 bottom](/assets/btn-1-general-tips-sticky-pads.webp)

## Mounting

The BTN-1 with the stand addon allows you to mount the device using M3 screws to attach using one of three mounting scenarios as shown below. It also allows for magnetic mounting from one orientation!

### **Under Desk**

![BTN-1 in its mount attached to the underside of a desk](/assets/btn-1-general-tips-under-desk-mount.jpg)

### Desk Mount

I used a block of wood instead of mounting it directly to my desk in this example.

![BTN-1 sliding into its mount screwed to a wooden block on a desk](/assets/btn-1-general-tips-desk-mount.webp)
