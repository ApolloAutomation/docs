# Reset Wi-Fi credentials on your BTN-1

Resetting the Wi-Fi credentials makes your device broadcast its hotspot again so you can enter new Wi-Fi details.

1\. Plug the BTN-1 into power.

![Plugging a USB-C cable into the port on the back of the BTN-1](/assets/btn-1-plug-in-usb-c.webp)

2\. Long press the Boot button (the button on the right) for 10 seconds.

![Back of the BTN-1 with arrows labeling the reset button on the left and the boot button on the right](/assets/btn-1-reset-boot-buttons.png)

3\. The Wi-Fi credentials should be reset and it will broadcast its hotspot again.

4\. Connect to the hotspot titled "Apollo BTN1 Hotspot" and enter your new Wi-Fi details!
