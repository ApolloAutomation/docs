# Teardown and reassembly of BTN-1

## Disassembly

You'll remove the lid, lift out the green PCB, and disconnect the battery.

!!! warning "If you have a BTN-1 with a battery you should flip the power switch to off"

    The BTN-1 ships with a lithium ion battery, however, this means it needs a master shutoff switch, which is on the left side of your BTN-1

1\. Flip the battery switch towards you to the off position. Users with no battery skip to step 2.

![Sliding the BTN-1 battery power switch on the side of the case to the off position](/assets/btn-1-flip-power-switch-off.webp)

2\. With the BTN-1 USB-C port facing upwards, unplug the USB-C cable and remove the lid.

![Unplugging the USB-C cable and prying the lid off the BTN-1 case with a pick](/assets/btn-1-remove-usb-c-lift-lid.webp)

3\. Gently lift the BTN-1 green PCB out of the device at an angle so the buttons clear the case easier.

![Lifting the BTN-1 circuit board out of the case at an angle, exposing the battery](/assets/btn-1-remove-pcb.webp)

4\. Remove the 2-pin JST connector and remove the battery.

![Unplugging the 2-pin JST battery connector from the BTN-1 circuit board](/assets/btn-1-unplug-jst.webp)

## Reassembly

To put everything back together, you'll reconnect the battery, seat the PCB, and snap the lid back on.

1\. Re-attach the 2-pin JST connector and place the battery writing side down with the wires on the left side of the case.

![Plugging the 2-pin JST battery connector back into the BTN-1 circuit board](/assets/btn-1-plug-in-jst.webp)

2\. Gently place the BTN-1 green PCB back in the case at an angle so the buttons will slide into cutout in the case.

![Lowering the BTN-1 circuit board back into the case at an angle over the battery](/assets/btn-1-replace-pcb.webp)

3\. Line up the lid of the case with the holes for the rgb leds then gently press down to snap it into place.

![Pressing the BTN-1 lid down over the keycaps until it snaps into place](/assets/btn-1-replace-lid.webp)

4\. Flip the power switch back on.

![Sliding the BTN-1 power switch on the side of the case to the on position](/assets/btn-1-flip-power-switch-on.webp)
