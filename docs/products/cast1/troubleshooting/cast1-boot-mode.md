# Putting the CAST-1 in boot mode

!!! info "This will cover how to put the CAST-1 into boot mode."

    This is needed to force the device into boot mode to reflash the firmware. Both methods do the same thing, so use whichever is easier for you.

## Method 1: Boot button only

1\. With the CAST-1 unplugged, hold down the **boot** button (on the left) while plugging a USB-C data cable into the CAST-1. Once connected, release the button.

![Holding the CAST-1 boot button while plugging in a USB-C cable](/assets/cast-1-boot-mode-method-1.webp)

2\. You are now ready to reflash your device!

[Click here to go to the reflashing guide!](https://wiki.apolloautomation.com/products/cast1/troubleshooting/cast1-reflash/){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## Method 2: Boot and reset buttons

This method puts your device into boot mode with the boot and reset buttons.

1\. Plug a USB-C cable that supports power and data into your computer and connect the other end to your CAST-1.

2\. Locate the boot and reset buttons on the CAST-1.

![CAST-1 case with labels pointing to the boot button on the left and the reset button on the right](/assets/cast-1-boot-reset-buttons.webp)

3\. Put your CAST-1 in boot mode. Use both buttons, in this order:

- Press and hold the **boot** button (on the left). Keep holding it.
- While you're still holding **boot**, press and release the **reset** button (on the right).
- Let go of **boot**.

!!! warning "Keep holding boot the whole time"

    Pressing **reset** by itself only restarts the CAST-1. It goes into boot mode only when **boot** is held down while you press and release **reset**.

![Animation with step captions: press and hold boot on the CAST-1, keep holding it while you tap reset, then let go of boot](/assets/cast-1-boot-mode-method-2.webp)

4\. You are now ready to reflash your device!
