# Reset Wi-Fi Credentials on your CAST-1

!!! note "Using the Ethernet module? Unplug the cable first."

    Your CAST-1 doesn't broadcast its hotspot while an Ethernet cable is connected, so take the cable out before you start.

1\. Plug the CAST-1 into power.

2\. Long press the **boot** button (on the left) for 10 seconds.

![](/assets/cast-1-boot-reset-buttons.webp)

3\. The Wi-Fi credentials should be reset and it will broadcast its hotspot again.

4\. Connect to the hotspot titled "**Apollo CAST 1 Hotspot**" and enter your new Wi-Fi details!

!!! tip "Still not broadcasting a hotspot?"

    If your CAST-1 won't broadcast its hotspot after resetting, [reflash it](https://wiki.apolloautomation.com/products/cast1/troubleshooting/cast1-reflash/) to restore the factory firmware and settings.
