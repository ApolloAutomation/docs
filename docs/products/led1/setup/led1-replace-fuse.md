# How to replace the 10 A Fuse

Follow these steps to open the case, swap in a new fuse, and close the case again.

1\. Gently pry open the lid. This can be a tight fit so you will want to use a small tool not your fingernail. Remove the lid and set aside.

![Tweezers prying the lid off the blue LED-1 case](/assets/led-1-pry-open-lid.jpg)![LED-1 board in the blue case with the lid removed and set aside](/assets/led-1-case-with-lid-off.jpg)

2\. Gently lift the LED-1 PCB out of the case. Be careful not to use a lot of force this could break components

![Fingers lifting the LED-1 board out of its blue case](/assets/led-1-lift-out-of-case.jpg)

3\. Flip the LED-1 PCB over and locate the replaceable fuse.

![Back of the LED-1 board with tweezers pointing to the 10 A fuse in its holder](/assets/led-1-pcb-back-point-to-fuse.jpg)

4\. Gently remove the fuse using tweezers.

![Tweezers gripping the fuse to pull it out of the LED-1 fuse holder](/assets/led-1-remove-fuse-1.jpg)![Tweezers lifting the old fuse clear of the LED-1 fuse holder](/assets/led-1-remove-fuse-2.jpg)

5\. Orient the new fuse with the writing facing up then place it inside the fuse bay.

![Tweezers placing a new fuse, writing facing up, into the LED-1 fuse bay](/assets/led-1-place-new-fuse-1.jpg)

6\. Gently push down on the fuse to make sure it is fully seated.

![Tweezers pressing the new fuse down until it is fully seated in the LED-1 fuse bay](/assets/led-1-place-new-fuse-2.jpg)

7\. Flip PCB over and angle it the same way you disassembled and rest the side of the pcb on the "shelf" as shown in the image below.

![LED-1 board angled back into the blue case, resting on the inner shelf](/assets/led-1-angle-back-in-case.jpg)

8\. Align the holes on the case so they line up with the Wago connectors.

![Lining up the blue lid openings with the Wago connectors on the LED-1](/assets/led-1-align-case-lid.jpg)

9\. Press down on the case lid to secure it to the bottom of the case.

![Thumb pressing the blue lid onto the LED-1 case, with RST, BT, and terminal labels](/assets/led-1-secure-lid-to-case.jpg)

10\. You're all done. Enjoy your LED-1!
