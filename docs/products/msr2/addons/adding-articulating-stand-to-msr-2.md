# Adding Articulating Stand To MSR-2

You'll swap the back of your sensor for the articulating stand backplate, then seat the stand's ball in its mount.

1\. Unplug your MSR-2 from power and remove the back.

![Back of the MSR-2 case with the Apollo logo backplate in place](/assets/msr-2-add-co2-pic-1.jpg)

![Pressing on the edge of the MSR-2 backplate to pop it off the case](/assets/msr-2-add-co2-pic-2.jpg)

![MSR-2 with the backplate removed, showing the board inside the case](/assets/msr-2-add-co2-pic-3-1.jpg)

2\. Align the new backplate that came with the articulating stand addon and gently push down on two corners near the articulating ball mount then the other two corners until it seats flat.

![Pressing the articulating stand backplate onto the back of the MSR-2 case](/assets/msr-2-stand-pic-1.jpg)

![Articulating stand backplate seated flat on the back of the MSR-2](/assets/msr-2-stand-pic-2.jpg)

3\. Align the ball inside the articulating mount and then push down until it's fully seated.

![Pushing the stand's ball into the articulating mount on the back of the MSR-2](/assets/msr-2-stand-pic-3.jpg)
