# Adding Rear Female USB-C To MSR-2

You'll seat the Female Rear USB-C addon on the mezzanine connector on the back of your sensor, then fit the new back that came with it.

1\. Unplug your MSR-2 from power and remove the back

![Back of the MSR-2 with the vented Apollo back cover still attached](/assets/msr-2-add-co2-pic-1.jpg)

![Squeezing the sides of the MSR-2 back cover to unclip it from the case](/assets/msr-2-add-co2-pic-2.jpg)

![MSR-2 with the back cover removed, exposing the circuit board](/assets/msr-2-add-co2-pic-3-1.jpg)

2\. Identify the "X" on the PCB on the back of your MSR-2 and the "X" on the top left corner of the Female Rear USB-C addon.

![Tool pointing to the X mark on the MSR-2 board, with the Female USB-C addon beside it](/assets/msr-2-female-usb-c-pic-1.jpeg)

![Close-up of the X mark on the Female USB-C addon next to the MSR-2 connector](/assets/msr-2-female-usb-c-pic-2.jpeg)

3\. Keep the "X" aligned on both sides and gently push down until it securely seats in the small mezzanine connector.

![Female USB-C addon seated in the MSR-2 mezzanine connector with the X marks aligned](/assets/msr-2-female-usb-c-pic-3.jpeg)

4\. Align the new back for your MSR-2 that came with the Female USB-C addon. It's possible you might have a flat back version and <a href="https://www.printables.com/model/932026-apollo-automation-msr-2-mmwave-co2-multisensor-for/files#preview.file.oGG7K" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer nofollow noopener">you can print that yourself too!</a>

![Holding the new MSR-2 back cover in place with the rear USB-C port poking through](/assets/msr-2-female-usb-c-pic-4.jpeg)

5\. Push down on the corners closest to the USB-C addon first, and then push the back on securely on the other two corners.

![MSR-2 with the new back cover attached and the rear USB-C port exposed](/assets/msr-2-female-usb-c-pic-5.jpeg)
