# MSR-2 Outlet Mount

You'll attach your sensor to a top facing USB-C plug with the Outlet Mount.

1\. Gather a top facing USB-C plug, an MSR-2, and our MSR-2 Outlet Mount.

![Top facing USB-C wall plug, MSR-2 Outlet Mount, and MSR-2 laid out together](/assets/msr-2-outlet-mount-pic-1.jpg)

2\. Align the MSR-2 with the USB-C port facing the left side and align the MSR-2 Outlet Mount as shown in the image below.

![MSR-2 Outlet Mount lined up with the USB-C port on the left side of the MSR-2](/assets/msr-2-outlet-mount-pic-2.jpg)

3\. Push the MSR-2 Outlet Mount into the USB-C port until it's fully seated. Do not push hard, it will go in very smoothly.

![MSR-2 Outlet Mount fully seated in the MSR-2 USB-C port](/assets/msr-2-outlet-mount-pic-3.jpg)

4\. Gently push the other USB-C port of the MSR-2 Outlet Mount into the top of the USB-C top facing plug.

![MSR-2 on the Outlet Mount, plugged into the top of the USB-C wall plug](/assets/msr-2-outlet-mount-pic-4.jpg)

!!! success "You're ready to plug it in and begin using your device!"
