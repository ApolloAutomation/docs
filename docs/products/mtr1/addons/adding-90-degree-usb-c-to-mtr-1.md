# Adding 90 degree USB-C to MTR-1

You'll attach this addon to your sensor and plug it into an outlet.

1\. Gather the MTR-1, 90 degree addon, and optional top facing USB-C Plug.

![MTR-1, 90 degree USB-C addon, and top facing USB-C wall plug laid out together](/assets/mtr-1-90-addon-pic-1.jpg)

2\. Insert the shorter end of the 90 degree addon into the MTR-1.

![Inserting the short end of the 90 degree addon into the MTR-1 USB-C port](/assets/mtr-1-90-addon-pic-2.jpg)

3\. Align the other end of the 90 degree addon to the optional top facing USB-C Plug.

![Long end of the 90 degree addon lined up with a top port on the USB-C wall plug](/assets/mtr-1-90-addon-pic-3.jpg)

4\. Gently push the connector into one of the top USB-C ports.

![90 degree addon pushed into the top USB-C port of the wall plug](/assets/mtr-1-90-addon-pic-4.jpg)

5\. Plug into an outlet and enjoy your sensor!

![MTR-1 on the 90 degree addon, plugged into a wall outlet through the USB-C plug](/assets/mtr-1-90-addon-final-image.webp)
