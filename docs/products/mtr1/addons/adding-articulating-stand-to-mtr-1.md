# Adding articulating stand to MTR-1

You'll swap the back of your sensor for the articulating stand backplate, then seat the stand's ball in its mount.

1\. Unplug your MTR-1 from power and remove the stand if attached.

![Back of the MTR-1 with the stand still attached beside the USB-C port](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-1.jpg)

2\. Remove the backplate of the MTR-1.

![MTR-1 with the backplate removed, showing the circuit board inside the case](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-4.jpg)

3\. Align the new backplate that came with the articulating stand addon and gently push down on two corners near the articulating ball mount then the other two corners until it seats flat.

![Pressing the articulating stand backplate onto the MTR-1 near the ball mount](/assets/mtr-1-stand-pic-1-1.jpg)

![Articulating stand backplate seated flat on the back of the MTR-1](/assets/mtr-1-stand-pic-2.jpg)

4\. Align the ball inside the articulating mount and then push down until it's fully seated.

![Pushing the articulating stand ball into the ball mount on the MTR-1](/assets/mtr-1-stand-pic-3.jpg)
