# Adding Rear male USB-C To MTR-1

You'll seat the Rear Male USB-C addon on the mezzanine connector on the back of your sensor, then fit the new back that came with it.

1\. Unplug your MTR-1 from power and remove the stand if attached.

![Back of the MTR-1 with the stand still attached beside the USB-C port](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-1.jpg)

2\. Remove the backplate of the MTR-1.

![MTR-1 with the backplate removed, showing the circuit board inside the case](/assets/mtr-1-add-co2-pic-4.jpg)

3\. Identify the "X" on the PCB on the back of your MTR-1 and the "X" on the top left corner of the male Rear USB-C addon.

![Tool pointing to the X mark on the MTR-1 board, with the Male USB-C addon beside it](/assets/mtr-1-add-male-usb-pic-1.jpg)

![Close-up of the X mark on the Male USB-C addon next to the MTR-1 connector](/assets/mtr-1-add-male-usb-pic-2.jpg)

4\. Keep the "X" aligned on both sides and gently push down until it securely seats in the small mezzanine connector.

![Male USB-C addon seated in the MTR-1 mezzanine connector with the X marks aligned](/assets/mtr-1-add-male-usb-pic-3.jpg)

5\. Align the new back for your MTR-1 that came with the Male USB-C addon. Push down on the corners closest to the USB-C addon first, and then push the back on securely on the other two corners.

![Pressing the new MTR-1 back on with the rear USB-C plug poking through](/assets/mtr-1-add-male-usb-pic-4.jpg)

6\. The case should sit flush and you're now ready to plug into a USB-C Outlet!

![MTR-1 with the new back sitting flush and the rear USB-C plug exposed](/assets/mtr-1-add-male-usb-pic-5.jpg)
