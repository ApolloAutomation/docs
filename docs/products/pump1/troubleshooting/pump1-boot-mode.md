# Putting the PUMP-1 in boot mode

!!! info "This will cover how to put the PUMP-1 into boot mode."

    This is needed to force the device into boot mode to reflash the firmware.

## Method 1: Boot button only

You'll hold the boot button while you plug in the USB-C cable.

!!! danger "Empty the water reservoir and make sure no water is present in the tubes"

    Make sure that there is no water which is able to damage the device. This is a precautionary measure to be safe!

1\. Disconnect your PUMP-1 from power. With the device facing you, locate the boot button. It’s the button on the right.

2\. Plug in a USB cable that supports power and data into your computer.

3\. Push and hold the boot button (the right button). While still holding the button down, plug in a USB-C cable into the USB-C port of your PUMP-1 then let go of the button.

![Holding the PUMP-1 boot button while plugging in the USB-C cable](/assets/pump-1-hold-boot-button-video.webp)

4\. You are now ready to reflash your device!

[Click here to go to the reflashing guide!](https://wiki.apolloautomation.com/products/pump1/troubleshooting/pump1-reflash/){    .md-button .md-button--primary }

## Method 2: Boot and reset buttons

You'll use the boot and reset buttons together while the device is plugged into your computer.

1\. Plug in a USB cable that supports power and data into your computer and connect the other end to your PUMP-1. With the device facing you, locate the reset (left) and boot (right) buttons.

![PUMP-1 with red arrows labeling the reset button on the left and the boot button on the right](/assets/pump-1-buttons-labeled.jpg)

2\. Put your PUMP-1 in boot mode. Use both buttons, in this order:

- Press and hold the **boot** button (the right button). Keep holding it.
- While you're still holding **boot**, press and release the **reset** button (the left button).
- Let go of **boot**.

!!! warning "Keep holding boot the whole time"

    Pressing **reset** by itself only restarts the PUMP-1. It goes into boot mode only when **boot** is held down while you press and release **reset**.

![Animation with step captions: press and hold boot on the PUMP-1, keep holding it while you tap reset, then let go of boot](/assets/pump-1-hold-boot-and-reset-button-video.webp)

3\. You are now ready to reflash your device!

[Click here to go to the reflashing guide!](https://wiki.apolloautomation.com/products/pump1/troubleshooting/pump1-reflash/){    .md-button .md-button--primary }
