# Install the LD2412 in R PRO-1

![Removing the R PRO-1 back, fitting the LD2412 onto its headers, and reassembling the case](/assets/r-pro-1-ld2412-install.webp)

!!! tip "Check out the gif above or step by step directions below!"

    The gif above shows all the steps needed to take apart and add your ld2412 to the R PRO-1 or you can follow the step by step tutorial with images below!

1\. Unplug your R PRO-1 from power and remove the back.

![Prying the R PRO-1 back plate loose from the case with tweezers](/assets/rpro-1-add-co2-lift-lid-1.webp)![Lifting the R PRO-1 back plate off the case by hand](/assets/rpro-1-add-co2-remove-lid.webp)

2\. Gently remove the PCB from the case and flip it over.

![R PRO-1 board out of its case, mmWave side up, with the LD2450 and LD2412 sensors](/assets/r-pro-1-no-case-mmwave-facing-up.webp)

3\. Place the LD2412 on the 4 and 3 pin headers (only fits one way).

![Lowering the LD2412 module onto its pin headers on the R PRO-1 board](/assets/r-pro-1-reassembly-install-ld2412.jpg)

4\. Place back into the case with the ethernet module facing up and the usb-c port facing you.

![R PRO-1 board seated in its case with the Ethernet port up and the USB-C port facing you](/assets/r-pro-1-lid-removed.jpg)

5\. Snap the lid back into place!

![Pressing the back plate down onto the R PRO-1 case until it snaps into place](/assets/r-pro-1-lid-press-down.jpg)
