# Add the gang box mounts to your R PRO-1

The gang box mounts let your Apollo R PRO-1 sit inside a 1-gang box on your wall, behind a blank faceplate. First you fit the two mounts onto the R PRO-1's board, then you install it in the box.

!!! note "Before you start"

    You need:

    * The two gang box mount pieces: one labeled "TOP" and one bottom mount.
    * A 1-gang box that's already attached to your wall.
    * A blank faceplate and its screws.

## Attach the mounts

Fit both mounts onto the board so the R PRO-1 is ready to go into the gang box.

1\. Unplug your R PRO-1 from power and remove the back.

![Prying the R PRO-1 back plate loose from the case with tweezers](/assets/rpro-1-add-co2-lift-lid-1.webp)![Lifting the R PRO-1 back plate off the case by hand](/assets/rpro-1-add-co2-remove-lid.webp)

2\. Gently lift the board (PCB) out of the case and set it face up with the USB-C port facing down.

![R PRO-1 board out of its case, face up, with the LD2450 and LD2412 mmWave sensors](/assets/r-pro-1-no-case-mmwave-facing-up.webp)

3\. Take the two gang box mount pieces and find the one labeled "TOP". The other piece is the bottom mount.

!!! danger "Check the orientation before you slide the mounts on"

    Make sure each mount is oriented exactly as shown in the images below. If the mounts aren't put on properly, the R PRO-1 could be damaged.

4\. Gently push the mount labeled "TOP" onto the top edge of the board, as shown below. Make sure its orientation is identical to the image.

![Clear gang box mount labeled TOP pushed onto the top edge of the R PRO-1 board](/assets/r-pro-1-gang-box-top-mount.jpg)

5\. Gently push the bottom mount onto the bottom edge of the board, below the LD2412, as shown below. Make sure its orientation is identical to the image.

![Clear bottom gang box mount pushed onto the bottom edge of the R PRO-1 board below the LD2412](/assets/r-pro-1-gang-box-bottom-mount.jpg)

6\. Compare your board with the image below. It should look identical.

![R PRO-1 board with the TOP mount on the top edge and the bottom mount on the bottom edge](/assets/r-pro-1-gang-box-mounts-installed.jpg)

Both mounts are on, and your R PRO-1 is ready to install inside a 1-gang box.

## Install in a 1-gang box

Now set the R PRO-1 into the box on your wall and cover it with the blank faceplate.

1\. Set the R PRO-1 into your 1-gang box (yours will be attached to the wall already) and line up the holes in its mounts with the holes in the box.

![R PRO-1 with mounts sitting in a 1-gang box, mount holes lined up with the box holes](/assets/r-pro-1-gang-box-line-up-holes.jpg)

2\. Put the screws through the blank faceplate and gently place it over the R PRO-1's mounting holes.

![Holding a blank faceplate with screws over the R PRO-1 mounted in a 1-gang box](/assets/r-pro-1-gang-box-install-faceplate.jpg)

3\. Screw in the screws slowly so you don't break the mounts or the blank faceplate.

![Blank white faceplate screwed onto the 1-gang box, covering the R PRO-1](/assets/r-pro-1-gang-box-face-plate-installed-finished.jpg)

Your R PRO-1 is now installed in the 1-gang box and should look like the image above.
