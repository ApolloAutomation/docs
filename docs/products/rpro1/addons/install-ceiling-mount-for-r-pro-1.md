## Attaching Mounts

1\. Unplug your R PRO-1 from power and remove the back.

![Small tool prying up the R PRO-1 back lid at the seam below the USB-C port](/assets/rpro-1-add-co2-lift-lid-1.webp)

![R PRO-1 back lid lifted off by hand, exposing the PCB inside the case](/assets/rpro-1-add-co2-remove-lid.webp)

2\. Gently lift the PCB out of the case and set it face up with the USB-C port facing down.

![R PRO-1 PCB out of its case, face up with the LD2450 and LD2412 mmWave sensors visible](/assets/r-pro-1-no-case-mmwave-facing-up.webp)

3\. Take the two ceiling mount pieces and identify the one labeled "TOP" and the other piece which is the bottom mount.

!!! danger "Before continuing and sliding the mounts on, please verify you have the pieces oriented as shown in the images below."

    If the mounts are not put on properly the R PRO-1 could be damaged!

3\. Gently push the top mount labeled "TOP" into the pcb as shown below. Make sure the orientation is identical to the image below!

![Ceiling mount piece labeled TOP pushed onto the top edge of the R PRO-1 PCB](/assets/r-pro-1-ceiling-box-top-mount.webp)

4\. Gently push the bottom mount into the pcb as shown below. Make sure the orientation is identical to the image below!

![Bottom ceiling mount piece pushed onto the R PRO-1 PCB edge below the LD2412 sensor](/assets/r-pro-1-ceiling-box-bottom-mount.webp)

5\. The end result should look identical to the image below. You are now ready to install it inside of the ceiling mount.

![R PRO-1 PCB with both the TOP and bottom ceiling mount pieces attached](/assets/r-pro-1-ceiling-box-mounts-installed.webp)

## Cut the Ceiling Hole

The Ceiling Mount needs a 3.5 inch (around 89mm) circular hole in the ceiling. Cut that hole before you install the mount. A [2 in. to 7 in. adjustable hole saw](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Milwaukee-2-in-to-7-in-Black-Oxide-Adjustable-Hole-Saw-Cutter-49-56-0320/205744284) set to 3.5 inches is what we used.

## Install in Ceiling Mount

1\. Remove the cover to expose the inside of the installed Ceiling Mount.

![Ceiling mount installed in the ceiling with its cover removed, showing the opening](/assets/r-pro-1-ceiling-mount-cover-removed.webp)

2\. Plug in Ethernet cable or USB-C cable.

![Ethernet cable plugged into the R PRO-1 with mounts attached, held at the ceiling mount](/assets/r-pro-1-ceiling-mount-plug-in-eth.webp)

3\. Gently slide R PRO-1 into the Ceiling Mount.

![R PRO-1 with the TOP mount upward being slid into the ceiling mount](/assets/r-pro-1-ceiling-mount-insert-r-pro-1.webp)

4\. Push the R PRO-1 back 1inch (around 2.5cm) to make sure the lip of the cover fits over the Ceiling Mount.

![R PRO-1 pushed back and recessed inside the ceiling mount](/assets/r-pro-1-ceiling-mount-recess-r-pro-1.webp)

5\. Gently press the ceiling mount cover until it is securely attached to the Ceiling Mount.

![Hand pressing the cover onto the ceiling mount until it is secured](/assets/r-pro-1-ceiling-mount-replace-cover.webp)
