# Mount your R PRO-1 high or on the ceiling

The LD2450 radar in your Apollo R PRO-1 can hang high on a wall or look down from the ceiling. The guide below sets up its zone map for each mount. The optional LD2412 needs nothing new: [calibrate it](apollo-mmwave-dashboard.md#calibrate-with-an-empty-room) where the sensor hangs. The LD2460 section is for other ESPHome devices with that radar.
