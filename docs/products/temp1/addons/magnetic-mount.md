# TEMP-1 Magnetic Mount

The TEMP-1 comes with an optional magnetic mount which can be used to secure the TEMP-1 to your fridge or grill as shown below.

![Correct TEMP-1 mounting on the grill side shelf versus incorrect mounting on the hot grill lid](/assets/temp1b-magnetic-mount-proper-mounting-explained-resized.png)

1\. To use your magnetic mount place it with the magnet facing upwards as shown:

![Empty TEMP-1 magnetic mount frame lying with the magnet facing up](/assets/temp1b-magnetic-mount-pic-1.jpg)

2\. Align the lid with the hole in the top right and place your TEMP-1 inside the magnetic mount and gently press down.

![TEMP-1 seated in the magnetic mount, seen from above with the magnet strip visible](/assets/temp1-magnetic-mount-installed-pic-1.jpg)

![Front view of the TEMP-1 seated in the magnetic mount above the magnet strip](/assets/temp1-magnetic-mount-installed-pic-2.jpg)

3\. It will sit flush as shown in the image below.

![Side view of the TEMP-1 sitting flush in the magnetic mount](/assets/temp1-magnetic-mount-installed-pic-3.jpg)

4\. Affix your TEMP-1 to the outside of fridge or a safe cool place on the grill.

![TEMP-1 in its magnetic mount attached to the outside of a black fridge](/assets/temp1-magnetic-mount-safe-fridge-mounting.jpg)

![TEMP-1 magnetic mount on the grill side shelf with the probe cable running into the lid](/assets/temp1b-magnetic-mount-safe-grill-mounting.jpg)

!!! danger "Do not leave your sensor outside or let it get wet!"

    The TEMP-1 should not be left outside for long periods of time or allowed to get wet. You will need to use another case around your TEMP-1 if there will be high moisture content in the air or if it is expected to rain.
