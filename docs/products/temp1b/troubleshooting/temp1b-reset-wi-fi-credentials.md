# Reset Wi-Fi credentials on your TEMP-1B

You'll reset the device's Wi-Fi credentials, then connect it to your new Wi-Fi network through its hotspot.

1\. Plug the device into power.

2\. Long press the Boot button for 10 seconds

![Tool pointing at the boot button opening on the side of the TEMP-1B case](/assets/temp-1b-boot-button-side-view-1.jpg)![TEMP-1B with the lid off, tool pointing at the BOOT button](/assets/temp-1b-boot-button-lid-off.jpg)

3\. The Wi-Fi credentials should be reset and it will broadcast its hotspot again.

4\. Connect to the hotspot titled "Apollo TEMP1B Hotspot" and enter your new Wi-Fi details!
